# 01: Facts ready on the catalogue card

**What to build:** Each product card in the catalogue shows one facts sentence under the title, decided with the rest of the product facts and printed from that outcome. The sentence is Facts unconfirmed when nothing is confirmed, even if a photo suggested a textile or not a textile. A complete textile pack is Facts ready. Explicit skips stay named, care first: Facts ready · Care skipped, Facts ready · GPSR skipped, or both. A filled care instruction or a filled GPSR identity adds no words, whether the identity is on the product or is a complete Shop GPSR identity. A product confirmed as not a textile leads with Not a textile, and never mentions care. When confirmed facts use Shop GPSR identity and that identity is no longer a GPSR identity — absent, incomplete, or gone with the Shopify connection — the sentence names GPSR identity and does not say Facts ready or Facts unconfirmed. A care skip or Not a textile still sits beside that hole. An explicit GPSR skip, or a complete product override, does not become that hole. Facts ready, including skips and Not a textile, is one calm line. Facts unconfirmed and Unfilled GPSR identity use the same amber as the confirm-facts line. Nothing in the sentence is red, and it never says the product complies with the law. The sentence hides while the card says Thinking. The top-right badge still says No copy, Synced, Pending, or Failed. “Confirm facts, then listing copy” stays only when the sentence is Facts unconfirmed and listing copy is missing. The product page, Website, Bulk SEO, Inventory, and New listing do not show it. Generate, Push, Go live, and Publish do not change. Stale listing copy stays off the sentence. Looking at it does not spend a Plan use.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] No confirmed facts, including a textile suggestion or a not-a-textile suggestion, reads Facts unconfirmed and amber, with no legal fact named
- [ ] A textile with fibre composition summing to 100 and care and GPSR filled reads Facts ready, calm, with no extra words
- [ ] Care skipped, GPSR skipped, and both skips read Facts ready · Care skipped, Facts ready · GPSR skipped, and Facts ready · Care skipped · GPSR skipped, calm
- [ ] A product confirmed as not a textile reads Not a textile · Facts ready when GPSR is filled, and Not a textile · Facts ready · GPSR skipped when GPSR is skipped, and never mentions care
- [ ] Shop GPSR identity that is complete counts as filled and adds no words; a product override that is complete stays Facts ready when Shop GPSR identity is absent
- [ ] Shop GPSR identity absent, incomplete, or gone with the Shopify connection, on a shop-default product, reads GPSR identity in amber, not Facts ready and not Facts unconfirmed
- [ ] That hole keeps Care skipped on a textile and Not a textile on a non-textile; an explicit GPSR skip does not become the hole
