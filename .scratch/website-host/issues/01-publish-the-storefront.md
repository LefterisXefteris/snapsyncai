# 01 — Publish the storefront

**What to build:** On the Website page the seller picks a palette (Ground, Ink, or Clay), a type pairing (sans, or a serif for the hero and product titles), and products already pushed with listing copy. They can preview. Publish puts the site on `{handle}.sites.snapsyncai.co.uk`. A shopper who is not signed in sees the shop name as the hero, the products in the order they were picked, and one page per product with the frozen listing copy, Channel photos, confirmed facts, and the SEO title and meta description. The first Publish that lands spends one Allowance use. A preview, a refused Publish, and a later Publish do not. The conversation can open the job and cannot Publish, and it no longer opens Lovable.
