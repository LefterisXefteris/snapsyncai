# Conversation

## Problem Statement

I want to ask SnapSync for what I already do in the catalogue — what is on hand, a listing-copy refresh, Bulk SEO, a website — the way I would ask a person. I want it to suggest a job I did not think of, and I want to turn that off. I do not want a second product that publishes to Shopify, invents fibre percentages, or replaces Products.

## Solution

I sign in as I do now. Products stays home once a Shopify shop is connected. **Conversation** is one dialogue for that shop, a destination in the same nav. If I have no Shopify shop, I arrive there and I am offered connect, which is the Shopify sign-in I already complete in the browser.

I can ask what Inventory is on hand, start Bulk SEO or listing copy refresh, and start a website prototype. Those are the same jobs the catalogue pages run. A proposal is visible before I accept. Accept is explicit, one product at a time, and it does not Push. For a website I write the look myself, and I confirm the handoff. A **job offer** can name Bulk SEO, listing copy refresh, or a website I did not ask for; it does not start the job. **Silence** is saved on the shop: stop offers, or stop one of those three jobs. Talk does not spend Allowance, and there is no cap on talk.

## Implementation Decisions

- One conversation module. The signed-in seller and a new message go in. A reply, the stored thread, silence, any open proposal, and sometimes a connect instruction come out. Callers do not learn how a job is run.
- The HTTP route in front of that module is thin. It authenticates the seller and passes the message through. It does not own job rules. Unauthenticated callers are refused.
- Silence is shop-level state on that dialogue: offers silenced, and each of Bulk SEO, listing copy refresh, and website silenced. Inventory and connect have no silence flag.
- The thread is stored on the shop. An open proposal (Bulk SEO pack, one-product refresh, or website choices not yet confirmed) is stored with it and survives reload.
- The model is a dependency of the module. Each turn it receives silence, any open proposal, and a recent stretch of the thread. Older turns stay stored for the seller and are not sent. The bound lives inside the module.
- The model never receives a Shopify admin token, a partner secret, or a shop domain it is allowed to choose. The module uses the seller’s connected shop.
- Job operations are the existing ones, called by the module, not reimplemented:
  - Bulk SEO: list eligibility, start, regenerate, accept, dismiss.
  - Listing copy refresh: propose, regenerate, accept, dismiss.
  - Inventory: read overview and items. No adjustment, no policy change, no bundle edit.
  - Website: read the prototype, hand off. The look is the seller’s words. Handoff is refused without a look, without a connected shop, or without at least one eligible product.
- Connect is an instruction to send the seller through the existing Shopify sign-in. The module does not complete OAuth and does not store a new token.
- Plan refusal, eligibility refusal, Allowance spend, and the first Overflow confirm of the month come from those jobs. The conversation surfaces their outcomes. It does not decide used-versus-included, and it does not confirm Overflow on the seller’s behalf.
- Talk does not call a spend. There is no dialogue meter and no cap.
- A job offer is a reply that names Bulk SEO, listing copy refresh, or a website. Starting that job requires a later seller message that agrees. Declining an offer stores the decline in the thread and does not set silence.
- Per-job silence means the module will not offer that job and will not call it, even if the seller asks. Offer silence means it will not volunteer a job, and it will still call a job the seller asked for, unless that job is silenced.
- While no Shopify shop is connected, the reply offers connect. It does not open a shop.
- The catalogue shell gains the Conversation destination. Products remains the home when a Shopify shop is connected. No shop routes the seller to Conversation. Existing pages are not removed and do not start calling the conversation module.
- Wix and Vinted are not inputs to this module.

## Testing Decisions

- Test the conversation module at its interface. A good test feeds a seller message and stored shop state, and asserts the reply, the stored silence and thread, whether a job was called, and the context the model received. It does not assert prompt wording or which private helper ran.
- The model and the four job modules are dependencies passed in. Tests use fakes. A fake job returns the same kinds of outcomes the real job already returns: a proposal, a refusal with a reason, a spent accept, an overflow confirm required, a mixed Bulk SEO pack.
- Do not re-prove search demand, fibre rules, Allowance arithmetic, snapshot contents, or Lovable URL shape here. Assert that the conversation called the job and passed the seller’s explicit accept, dismiss, regenerate, or handoff confirm through, including an overflow confirm when the job asked for one.
- Assert these behaviours at that one interface: inventory is read and never adjusted; a silenced job is not called; an offer does not call a job; a decline does not set silence; talk does not spend; no look means no handoff; the model input contains silence, an open proposal, and the recent stretch, and omits an older stored turn; a shop domain in the message does not switch shops; no Shopify shop yields a connect instruction and no job call.
- The route test only checks that the dialogue is authenticated. Prior art is the Bulk SEO route tests. Behaviour prior art is the Bulk SEO module tests and the website handoff module tests.
- Existing Bulk SEO, listing copy refresh, Inventory, website, and Plan tests stay the proofs of those jobs.

## Out of Scope

- Channel claim. Opening a Shopify shop, confirming a shop name and email, and a claim link wait until Shopify grants that API. This spec only offers connect when there is no shop.
- A SnapSync publication on the shop. Go live stays the Online Store, on the product page.
- New listing, first listing-copy generate, confirming fibre composition, care instructions, or GPSR identity, and go live, from the conversation.
- Push, inventory adjustments, and any route the dialogue was not given.
- Wix and Vinted inside the conversation.
- More than one thread per shop.
- Metering or capping talk.
- Passing Shopify credentials to Lovable, a live read for Lovable, or any MCP.
- Replacing the catalogue pages, or making Conversation the home once a shop is connected.
