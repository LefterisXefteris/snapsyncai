# The catalogue stays home

Products stays the home once a Shopify Channel exists. The **conversation** is a destination beside the catalogue. A seller with no Shopify Channel arrives in the conversation each time they open the workspace itself, to claim a shop or connect one they already own. A destination they open stays until they leave the workspace: the catalogue, a product, Inventory, Import, New listing, the website, Bulk SEO, or Settings. Disconnecting, and a connect or Channel claim trip, are not leaving. They come back to the place they left, and a failed connect is told there. The next open of the workspace is Products when a Shopify Channel exists, and the conversation when it does not. A second window is another open. From the conversation the seller may start a Channel claim or connect, ask what Inventory is on hand, start Bulk SEO or listing copy refresh, and start a website prototype. New listing stays on its canvas. Fibre composition, care instructions, and GPSR identity stay on the product page, and the conversation must not invent them. Go live stays on the product page. A **job offer** may name a job the seller did not ask for; the job starts only when they agree. We rejected a conversation-only shell, we rejected locking the catalogue until a shop exists, and we rejected an offer that starts the run.
