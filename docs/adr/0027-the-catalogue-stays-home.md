# The catalogue stays home

Products stays the home once a Shopify Channel exists. The **conversation** is a destination in that nav. A seller with no Shopify Channel arrives in the conversation, to claim a shop or connect one they already own. From the conversation the seller may start a Channel claim or connect, ask what Inventory is on hand, start Bulk SEO or listing copy refresh, and start a website prototype. New listing stays on its canvas. Fibre composition, care instructions, and GPSR identity stay on the product page, and the conversation must not invent them. Go live stays on the product page. A **job offer** may name a job the seller did not ask for; the job starts only when they agree. We rejected a conversation-only shell, and we rejected an offer that starts the run.
