# Chat stays along the bottom

**Chat** is always along the bottom of the content area in the signed-in workspace. It overlays the open Destination. The seller does not open or close it. A Choose photos sentence stacks in the panel until the page reloads. That sentence is still not a turn, and it is still not stored on the shop thread.

This supersedes the card the seller opens in [0050](./0050-chat-is-a-card-beside-the-api.md), and the lines in [0051](./0051-choose-photos-may-start-from-chat.md) that open the card on click and keep a photo sentence until the card closes. [0050](./0050-chat-is-a-card-beside-the-api.md) still holds for the API, the acts, Credits, and one thread per shop. [0051](./0051-choose-photos-may-start-from-chat.md) still holds for draft products, Group, and the acts that stay in the browser.

We rejected a corner card the seller opens and closes, a card kept open at the bottom right, and a shelf across the bottom.
