# SnapSync hosts the Website

Supersedes [0010](./0010-website-is-a-lovable-snapshot-handoff.md). The palette prototype, and the rejection of a generated look, are superseded by [0035](./0035-the-website-agent-builds-the-look.md). Hosting, Publish, checkout, and the Lovable rejection stand.

A **Website** is hosted by SnapSync for one Shopify shop, at `{handle}.sites.snapsyncai.co.uk`, using that shop's myshopify handle. The address is not the workspace. The storefront does not read the seller's sign-in, and the API does not treat that host as the workspace. The seller Publishes from a **Website prototype**: a palette (Ground, Ink, or Clay), a type pairing (sans throughout, or a serif for the hero and product titles), and products already pushed with listing copy. The layout is a hero with the shop name, a grid in the order they picked, and one page per product. That page shows the published listing copy, photos, and confirmed facts, and uses the published SEO title and meta description when they exist. Photos stay the Channel's URLs ([0018](./0018-snap-sync-photos-are-private-channel-photos-are-not.md)). Words and photos freeze at Publish. Price, compare-at, and Buy come from the shop: Buy is missing unless the product is Active on the Online Store at a price greater than zero. The shopper picks a variant when there is more than one, and a quantity starting at 1, on a cart the site holds. Checkout is the shop's, and SnapSync does not take payment. The first Publish that lands spends one Allowance use. A preview, a refused Publish, and every later Publish do not. Taking the site down keeps the address. The conversation can open the job; Publish stays with the seller. v1 is Shopify-only. A custom domain is not in this version.

We rejected Lovable, because the seller would have to subscribe and SnapSync cannot pay Lovable's credits for them. We rejected a bespoke generated layout, a Shopify theme, hosting on the workspace origin, and hiding a product that can no longer be bought.
