# Product kind selects the facts pack

SnapSync’s facts gate was a boolean `isTextile`: textiles required fibre and care, and “not a textile” opened listing copy with almost no facts. That escape hatch would invent food claims the same way photos once invented fibre. We introduce **Product kind** (Textile product | Coffee product): the seller picks kind on New listing; each kind has its own confirmed-facts pack before generate, refresh, or Bulk SEO. Coffee product uses a food-label pack (no GPSR, Shopify Push only); capsule compatibility stays Classification/product type, not a gate. Extending the boolean was rejected so accessories and later kinds do not pile into “other.” Existing confirmed non-textile products must pick a kind and confirm that pack before a new generate, refresh, or Bulk SEO run.
