# Free workspace; listing-copy writes at £0.70 after three free

Superseded by [0049](0049-prepaid-credits-replace-per-write-charge.md) for the money meter (Credits replace £0.70 per write). Free workspace scope and the three-free onboarding idea still inform 0049.

The sellable product is no longer a **Plan**. The workspace is free for every seller — catalogue, facts, typed listing copy, Push, Import, Inventory, Classification, talk, and **Website** (prototype and Publish). Money is only for a **listing-copy write**: one **Accept** that lands for first generate, listing copy refresh, or one Bulk SEO product. Each shop gets **three free** writes for life, then **£0.70 GBP** per write when it lands. SEO and AEO are fields inside listing copy, not separate prices. A per-field regenerate after an Accept is another write; a failed generate and a stale regenerate are not. The first paid write on a shop needs the seller's confirm and a card; later paid writes on that shop charge silently when the Accept lands; a failed charge means the Accept does not save. Existing Plan and Leftover weekly run to the end of the paid period with today's inclusion, do not renew, and are not refunded for time already paid; then that shop follows these write rules. New checkouts are not a Plan and not Leftover weekly.

This supersedes the Plan-as-product and inclusion model in [0014](0014-plan-and-allowance-not-weekly-cap.md), [0020](0020-overflow-is-seventy-pence.md), [0021](0021-overflow-confirm-first-extra.md), and [0039](0039-the-plan-has-no-use-count.md). Talk still does not meter ([0032](0032-conversation-talk-does-not-spend-allowance.md)); the meter is listing-copy writes, not Allowance.

**Considered:** keeping Plan as optional unlimited AI beside free+70p (rejected — two products forever); 70p only as Overflow on a Plan (rejected — that was ADR 0020, and it blocks “the app is free”); prepaid packs or a balance (rejected — credits stay dead); a monthly open invoice of 70p lines (rejected — Plan billing without the name); field-level SEO/AEO prices (rejected — one Accept is one listing copy); Website Publish as a write (rejected — only gen listing-copy features charge); lifetime three free per seller across shops (rejected — Plan’s shop boundary); confirm every paid write (rejected — a photoshoot weekend is not dozens of dialogs); save the Accept when the charge fails (rejected — no free AI on a dead card); cutting active Plan mid-period or forever-grandfathering Plan (rejected — finish what was paid, one end state).
