# One conversation per shop, and silence

Each shop has one **conversation**. **Silence** is a shop-level choice on it: stop job offers, or stop the conversation from offering or starting Bulk SEO, listing copy refresh, or a website prototype. Inventory answers, a Channel claim, and connect cannot be silenced. Silencing a job leaves its catalogue page in place. The conversation destination stays, including for a seller with no Shopify Channel. We rejected a pile of threads, and we rejected removing the catalogue page or the conversation itself.
