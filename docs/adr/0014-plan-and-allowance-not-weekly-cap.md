# Plan and Allowance, not a weekly product cap

SnapSync used to sell a weekly fee with a hard 30-product cap (and, before that, credit packs). That stacked a time charge with rationing: quiet sellers overpaid per listing-copy write, busy sellers hit a wall, and unused weeks still billed. Money now buys one **Plan** at **£19/month** or **£190/year**, with **20 Allowance uses each calendar month** (listing-copy writes and website); extra uses are **£1.50** on the Plan invoice, not a pack and not a stop. Annual is a cheaper fee for the same monthly Allowance, not 240 uses in one bucket. A use spends only when the write or website lands. The free workspace stays usable without a card. Existing £4/week checkouts stay until the seller cancels or switches; new checkouts get this Plan. We rejected selling a compliance check, metering legal facts, charging to regenerate stale listing copy, two paid tiers, a yearly Allowance bank, silent-migrating weekly subscribers onto overage, and going back to credits.
