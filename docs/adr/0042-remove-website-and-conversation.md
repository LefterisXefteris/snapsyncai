# Website and Conversation leave the product

The seller workspace no longer has a **Website** Destination (SnapSync-hosted storefront, prototype, agent, or Publish) or a **Conversation** Destination (shop dialogue, Silence, or Job offers). Accept, Bulk SEO, and listing copy refresh stay on the catalogue, product page, and Bulk SEO. Connect and Channel claim stay on Products and Settings. Live Published sites stop being served; prototype, publish, conversation, and silence data are dropped with the deploy. This supersedes ADRs 0010, 0025–0038 (and Website/Conversation mentions in 0018, 0027, 0032, 0039–0041) for product truth; those ADRs stay as history.

We rejected keeping the Destinations as stubs, UI-only removal with live backends, and inventing a new Destination to replace Conversation’s job offers.
