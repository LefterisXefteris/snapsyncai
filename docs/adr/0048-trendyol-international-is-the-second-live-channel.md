# Trendyol International is the second live Channel

SnapSync’s live Channels are Shopify and Trendyol International. Wix and Vinted remain Channel names in the glossary but are not connectable until their adapters land as tickets under [0013](./0013-channel-adapters-land-with-the-second-channel.md). This supersedes the “one live Channel (Shopify)” sentence in 0013; the in-repo adapter rule and rejection of a plugin registry stand.

Trendyol International has its own connect, Trendyol listing, and Push. Import, Inventory, publications, Draft/Active, Shop GPSR, and listing-copy write metering stay Shopify-shaped. A Coffee product may Push only to Shopify. We rejected pretending all four Channels are live on the landing page, and rejected dropping Trendyol from the public claim while connect and Push remain in the product.
